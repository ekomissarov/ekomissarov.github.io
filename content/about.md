I'm a product and data analyst with a focus on experimentation, statistical methods, and causal inference.

My work revolves around understanding how digital products perform, designing reliable experiments, and turning data into evidence for product decisions.

## Areas of Expertise

- **Product Analytics** — product metrics, user behavior, funnels, retention, and engagement.
- **Experimentation** — A/B testing, experimental design, variance reduction, and statistical inference.
- **Causal Inference** — quasi-experimental methods and causal impact evaluation.
- **Analytics Engineering** — data pipelines, analytical workflows, and experiment quality assurance.

## Technical Interests

I'm particularly interested in the engineering challenges behind reliable experimentation:

- Experimentation platform architecture
- Randomization and experiment assignment
- Statistical methods and variance reduction
- Data quality and experiment monitoring
- Scalable analytical systems
