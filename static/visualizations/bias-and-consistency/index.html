Bias and consistency are different properties

Each panel shows the distribution of one estimator of μ over repeated samples of size n from N(μ, 1), with μ = 0. Bias is the estimator’s mean minus the true μ. The distance between the dashed and solid lines shows the magnitude of the bias. Spread is the width of the curve. The horizontal axis is the same in all panels.
