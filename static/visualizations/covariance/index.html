The idea behind the formula

Each point i and the sample center (x̄, ȳ) are opposite corners of a rectangle with sides |xi − x̄| and |yi − ȳ|. The product (xi − x̄)(yi − ȳ) is that rectangle's area with a sign: + (green) when both deviations have the same sign, − (red) when they differ. Covariance is the average of these signed areas. If Y = X every rectangle becomes a square and covariance turns into the variance.

Dividing by σXσY removes the units and gives correlation, which always lies between −1 and 1. The sliders set the true ρ, σ and μ; the points are a random sample from that distribution, so the sample values on the panel only approximate them (typical error of corr is about 1/√n).
